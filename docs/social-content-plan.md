Current priority, confirmed by David on November 13, 2026: launch the website and produce frequent videos, aiming for nearly daily uploads. Petre and Stefan own content; David supplies software captures and technical checks. Social platforms, handles, posting operator and approval workflow are not yet confirmed. This plan does not authorize an assistant to publish posts.

## Lightweight production loop

1. Petre and Stefan choose one understandable idea and draft a short script, caption and shot list.
2. David confirms which software behavior is implemented and supplies a clean capture where useful. Ask Tulica for educational review where appropriate; do not assume an approval already exists.
3. Record/edit a vertical master, add readable Romanian captions and a clear ending directing viewers to the website. Crop and text placement should be checked in the chosen platform's actual preview.
4. Keep two or three finished clips ready so nearly daily posting does not require daily production. Reuse an approved visual template, not the same script.
5. The designated human approves and uploads each clip. Record its URL, date and outcome in the table below; keep account credentials private.
6. Use questions and observable response to plan the next batch. Do not invent viewer counts or learning results.

## Before publishing

- Use original, licensed or platform-authorized footage/music; retain any required credits.
- Get permission before publishing identifiable people, voices, school material or private screens. Hide notifications and local credentials in captures.
- Label renders and proposed capabilities clearly. Do not present website narration as live AI, or a reflection concept as proof of a built physical prototype.
- Check captions, audio, the final link and the platform preview. Real-phone website checks remain deferred by David; do not mark them complete just because a clip looks correct.
