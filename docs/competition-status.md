## What the public rules allow now

Eligibility, as printed: a student enrolled at a school in Romania, aged 16 to 19. A team may have 2 to 5 members and must include one coordinating teacher. The teacher submits the team entry and is the organizer's contact. On November 17 the user confirmed the entry: Bordea David, Oprea Petre Teodor and Cheslerean Stefan, all 16–19 and enrolled at the school, with Dr. Ing. Prof. Alexandru Constantin Tulica as the named coordinating teacher. That is four people, inside the printed maximum. The form and the school permission were not copied into this repository. The user also confirmed that the school has given permission for the team photograph and the school references.

Participation is free. The four printed themes are education and diversity, environmental protection, social change through sport, and financial education, each through technology. Projects outside those themes can be accepted or refused at the organizer's discretion. HoloCAD is an education project. The theme selected on the form was not inspected.

Ideas must be original. Any part generated with artificial intelligence must be identified as such. The website already says that the product visuals and narration use AI, and that local AI assistance is not validated.

The competition name may be used to say the project is entered in Samsung Solve for Tomorrow. The rules do not provide a license to use the Samsung logo, product images, or language that says Samsung endorses HoloCAD. The website and the brand frames use the competition name in text. They do not include the Samsung logo.

For the Social Media Ambassador consideration, the rules name public posts on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok that mention Samsung Solve for Tomorrow and use all three hashtags each time: `#SFTRomania2026`, `#SFTRO26` and `#SFT_ROMANIA_2026`. Posts count until 12:00 on the final day. The existing brand frames include the competition name and `#SFTRomania2026` only. Who posts, and from which accounts, is still a decision for David, Petre and Stefan. The site already links to `https://www.instagram.com/holocad26/`.

## What waits until selection

Stage 2 is only for the 25 projects the jury selects. The PDF schedules judging for November 12–25, contact with selected teams during November 26–December 10, and publication of the finalist list on December 18. The public calendar instead says the 25 finalists are announced on December 11, 2026. Use the organizer's actual email if those dates differ.

Selected teams are asked for identification, parental consent, and image consent. Those forms stay out of this repository. A prototype and a test with the intended audience are final-stage judging criteria, not current work. Workshops, mentoring, Demo Day and the Bucharest final on March 12, 2027 also belong to that later stage.

Organizer contact printed in the rules: `sft@publicadvisors.ro`. Do not send a new registration. Reply only when the organizer writes, and keep that correspondence private.
